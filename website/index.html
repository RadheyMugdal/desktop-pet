01 Built to stay out of your way

It has a home spot

Tabby lives in the bottom-right corner by default and only takes a few steps now and then. Drag it anywhere and that becomes its new home. If you'd like it to wander, turn on Roam freely.

Clicks go straight through

The pet only catches the mouse when you point right at it. Everywhere else, clicks reach your windows, icons and taskbar as if it weren't there.

Light as a nap

About 1% of one CPU core while it sits. It hides and stops completely while you game or watch fullscreen video. It's built on Tauri, not Electron.

Interactive play & food

Drop a bouncy yarn ball for your cat to chase and bat across the screen, or serve a full food bowl from the tray menu.

Focus timer & breaks

Start a Pomodoro focus session. Your pet sits quietly beside a desk clock, then gently suggests a stretch when it's break time.

Smart auto-rest & audio

Senses when you're busy typing and curls up quietly. Gentle chirps, friendly trills, and soft purrs with zero annoying drones.

04 Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no account and no ads.

Does it collect anything?

No. The app never connects to the internet. It only checks where your mouse is, to know when you're pointing at the pet, and that never leaves your computer.

Why does macOS say “Apple cannot verify that this app is free of malware”?

macOS Gatekeeper displays this warning for any open-source app downloaded outside the Mac App Store that isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID. Desktop Pet is 100% open-source, safe, and privacy-respecting. You can open it by right-clicking the app and selecting Open, or by running xattr -cr /Applications/Desktop\ Pet.app in Terminal.

How do I play with the yarn ball or feed my pet?

Right-click your pet or click the cat icon in your system tray to drop an interactive yarn ball or place a food bowl. You can drag and flick the yarn ball across the screen! Double-click the yarn ball anytime to put it away.

How does the Focus timer work?

Choose Focus & reminders in the tray menu. Start a 25-minute Pomodoro session, and your pet will quietly keep you company with a little desk clock, then notify you when it's time for a 5-minute break.

How do I change pets, colors or outfits?

Right-click your pet, or click the cat icon in your system tray (Windows and Linux) or menu bar (macOS), and use Pet, Color and Outfit. It remembers your choices.

It's in my way. How do I move it?

Drag it anywhere and drop it. That spot becomes its new home. If it ever goes missing, choose Bring pet back in the tray menu.

Does it remind me to take breaks?

Yes, gently. After about 50 minutes of work, your pet suggests a stretch in a little speech bubble. Pet it to say "got it". Step away for a few minutes and it naps until you're back. You can turn reminders off in the menu.

Will it slow down my games?

No. When a game or video goes fullscreen, the pet hides and stops animating until you're back (on Windows today, with macOS and Linux coming next).

How do I get new versions?

It updates itself. When a new version is ready, the menu shows Restart to update. It starts with your computer too, and you can switch that off in the menu.

How do I quit or uninstall it?

Choose Quit in the tray menu. Uninstall it like any other app: Settings → Apps on Windows, drag it to the Trash on macOS, or remove the package on Linux.
